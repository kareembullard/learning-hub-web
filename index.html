🎧 Learning & Media Tracker

Organize podcasts, YouTube channels, courses, and books into one library with progress tracking.

This is a portfolio demo. All data (library items, streaks, goals) is fictional sample data, seeded on first load. Everything is editable — add/edit/delete library items, update progress and status, and adjust your profile settings. Edits save to your browser only (localStorage); nothing is ever sent to a server. Use Reset in the toolbar to restore the original demo data.
